Video Recorder

Privacy Policy

Effective September 11, 2026 · Publisher: backrt

Video Recorder processes recordings locally in Microsoft Edge. It has no backend service and does not send recorded content or usage information to the publisher or third parties.

Information processed locally

After the user explicitly starts recording, the extension processes video and audio currently played by HTML media elements in the selected tab. It may use the page title to create a local filename. Recording chunks and temporary WebM files remain in browser-local temporary storage while the recording is active or being finalized.

The extension locally stores recording state, acknowledgement of the usage notice, and up to 100 diagnostic events and error messages. Diagnostic logs are replaced when a new recording starts.

Data transmission and sharing

The extension does not transmit recorded video, audio, page content, URLs, browsing history, diagnostics, identifiers, or usage data. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, accounts, payments, or extension-provided cloud synchronization.

Storage and retention

Temporary chunks are kept only while a recording is processed and are removed after finalization or cleanup. The finished WebM file is saved to the user’s downloads folder. Local settings and diagnostics remain until replaced, cleared by the user, or removed when the extension is uninstalled.

Permissions

Permissions are used only to find video elements after a user request, run packaged recording code, store temporary chunks, finalize seekable WebM files, save downloads, handle bounded timeouts, and retain local settings and diagnostics.

Protected content and responsibility

The extension does not retrieve original media or bypass DRM, subscriptions, paywalls, access controls, or website protections. Users should record only content they created, are authorized to save, is in the public domain, or may otherwise lawfully record.

Children’s privacy

Video Recorder is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information.

Changes and contact

The effective date identifies the latest revision. For privacy questions or support, visit GitHub Issues.

视频录制机

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 11 日 · 发布者：backrt

视频录制机只在 Microsoft Edge 本地处理录制数据。本扩展没有后端服务，不会把录制内容或使用信息发送给发布者或任何第三方。

本地处理的信息

用户主动开始录制后，本扩展会处理所选标签页中 HTML 媒体元素实际播放的视频和音频，并可能使用页面标题生成本地文件名。录制分片和临时 WebM 文件只会在录制或终结处理期间保存在浏览器本地临时存储中。

扩展还会在本地保存当前录制状态、用户对使用须知的确认，以及最多 100 条诊断事件和错误信息。开始新的录制时，旧诊断日志会被替换。

数据传输与共享

本扩展不会传输录制的视频、音频、页面内容、网址、浏览历史、诊断日志、标识符或使用数据，也不包含分析、广告、跟踪、账号、支付或由扩展提供的云同步。

存储与保留

临时分片只在处理录制任务期间保留，并在终结或清理后删除。最终 WebM 文件保存在用户的下载目录中。本地设置和诊断信息会保留到被替换、由用户清除或卸载扩展。

权限用途

权限只用于在用户请求后查找视频元素、运行随扩展打包的录制代码、保存临时分片、生成可定位的 WebM 文件、保存下载、处理有时限的结束等待，以及保留本地设置和诊断信息。

受保护内容与用户责任

本扩展不会获取原始媒体，也不会绕过 DRM、订阅、付费墙、访问控制或网站保护。请仅录制自己创作、已经获得授权、属于公有领域或法律允许录制的内容。

儿童隐私

视频录制机不以儿童为目标用户，也不会有意收集个人信息。

变更与联系方式

页面顶部的生效日期代表最新修订时间。如有隐私或支持问题，请访问 GitHub Issues。
